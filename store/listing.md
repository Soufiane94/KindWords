## English

**App name:** Kindwords

**Short description:**
Gentle kind words, now and then. No account, and nothing leaves your phone.

**Full description:**

Kindwords sends you a gentle kind word now and then: on an ordinary day,
the evening before an exam, the morning of a hospital appointment, or on a
day that feels heavy. It's made for anyone who feels alone, is grieving,
is unwell, or is simply tired.

What it does
• Kind words chosen for you. Tell Kindwords a little about what you're going through (studying, living alone, grieving, caring for someone, far from home…) and it picks words that fit.
• At your own pace. Once a day, three times a week or at the times you choose, with quiet hours. If you stay away for a while, it gently slows down. No streaks, no "we miss you".
• Around the days that matter. Add an exam, an interview or an anniversary, and a kind word comes before and after.
• Notes for you. Write to yourself or to future you, or keep a message from someone you love. They come back to you now and then.
• Send a kind word. Pass one on to a friend as a picture or as text, through your phone's own share menu.
• Make it feel like home. A home screen widget, 14 worlds from plain and calm to cozy cabin or soft clouds, a plain-font option, and support for large text and screen readers.
• "Need to talk to someone?" A quiet page with listening lines, there if you need it.

Nothing leaves your phone
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your notes, your answers and your settings are kept on your phone only, and nothing is ever sent to us. Something only leaves your phone when you choose to send it.

Kindwords is not a medical service. If you're in danger, please call your local emergency number.
